Community guidelines

Be kind, stay focused, protect your privacy.

  • Use moderated topic-based study rooms.
  • Never share passwords, address or phone number publicly.
  • Report inappropriate messages and block unwanted contact.
  • For younger students, private messaging should be disabled by default.

WhatsApp / Telegram invite links can be added by an administrator after safety and privacy review.
